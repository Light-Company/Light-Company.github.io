## Platform
web

## Users
Robotics researchers, lab operators and technical leaders evaluating robot policies in physical workcells. Investors, prospective colleagues and partners need a clear account of the company and its product. Prism IRL has a separate audience and remains at /prism.

## Product Purpose
Prism brings projected instructions and camera-based observation to physical work. For evaluation, its core story is stage, verify, record and revisit a scene so a policy result retains its context.

## Operating Context
Real projectors, cameras, calibrated tables, physical objects, human operators and robot arms. Evidence: the public technical brief, recorded lab films, and the local prism-nt-designer source. A rendered concept does not establish production availability.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Projected placement guidance and camera registration are evidenced. The technical brief describes per-episode records and re-projection. Full scene branching, comparison timelines, collaborative review and automatic regression orchestration are proposed product direction, not independently verified shipping capabilities. Placement tolerances, lighting control, deformable objects and downstream scoring need task-specific validation. Benchmark data remains illustrative.

## Product Principles

- Explain the physical mechanism before the platform abstraction.
- Preserve uncertainty and evidence alongside results.
