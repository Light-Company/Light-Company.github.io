# Organic search and distribution campaign

## Objective and measurement
Help a robotics team understand Prism and start a qualified workcell conversation. Primary outcome: inquiries that describe a real robot, task and evaluation problem. Secondary outcomes: guide discovery, technical-brief use and returning readers.

No Search Console, keyword-volume or analytics account was available for this audit. Search volume, ranking difficulty, current positions, conversion rates and traffic targets are deliberately not invented. Use the intent map below as a hypothesis, then adjust it with observed queries and inquiry quality.

## Implemented technical work
- Unique page titles, descriptions, one H1, canonical URLs, social metadata and visible main content in server/static HTML.
- Organization, WebPage, BreadcrumbList and Article metadata matching visible content. No fake reviews, ratings, job postings or result datasets in schema.
- A sitemap limited to canonical, indexable pages. The illustrative benchmark and scene-history concept remain noindex; investor pages retain their existing noindex setting and are omitted from the sitemap. /demo is an alias, not a separate canonical entry.
- Useful navigation and footer links from existing product and investor pages; Prism Robotics and Prism IRL keep distinct destinations.
- Local fonts, deferred enhancement scripts, user-controlled video on new editorial pages and reduced-motion behavior.
- Public llms.txt, llms-full.txt and agents.md mirror the product distinctions. These are optional reading aids, not search-ranking promises.

## Distribution discipline
Drafts live in social-copy.md. For each guide, prepare a short post plus a longer founder note. Link to the useful resource, not always the homepage. Use the actual lab footage when its caption explains what is visible. Request permission before identifying a partner or publishing its data.

No messages or social posts have been sent. No ads, link purchases or budget commitments are part of this release. Avoid mass directory submission and automated outreach unrelated to the reader's actual work.

## Measurement setup when access is available
1. Verify the domain in Search Console using the owner's authorized account and DNS process.
2. Submit /sitemap.xml and inspect the main product page and resource hub.
3. Review indexing, query impressions, clicks and destination pages weekly. Separate branded and non-branded demand.
4. Track qualified inquiries in the existing business process; ask which guide helped. Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
5. If analytics is later added, define events and privacy choices first. Suggested events: product_contact_click, checklist_download and station_interest_success. These are a proposed taxonomy, not currently installed tracking.

## Audit limits
Build checks and browser review verify website behavior and markup. They do not establish search-engine indexing, ranking improvements, field Core Web Vitals or complete accessibility conformance. No numerical performance claim is made before those measurements exist.
