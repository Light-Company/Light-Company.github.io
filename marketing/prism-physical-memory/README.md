# Give the physical world a memory

An organic content campaign for The Light Company / Prism Robotics, prepared November 1, 2026.

## The campaign
For robotics teams who cannot explain whether a changed result came from the policy or its physical setup, Prism makes scene preparation more explicit through projected guidance and camera-based checks. The larger product direction is a history of scenes that remains useful to the next person in the lab.

Primary action: discuss a workcell at https://lightcompany.ai/contact. Secondary action: register station interest at https://lightcompany.ai/order. Neither action purchases a product.

## Delivered
- A substantial Prism Robotics page and a dedicated workflow explanation.
- A visibly labeled interactive scene-history concept, excluded from indexing.
- Four educational guides, a resource hub and a downloadable evaluation checklist.
- Company, careers, contact, trust, terms, privacy, cookies and accessibility pages.
- Shared site navigation/footer, canonical metadata, Article/WebPage/Organization/Breadcrumb structured data, a canonical sitemap and AI-readable summaries.
- An eight-week editorial/distribution plan, keyword map, source notes and ready-to-edit social copy in this kit.

## Voice
Expansive and physical. Begin with the cup, the camera, the table or the person doing the work. Explain the mechanism before the abstraction. Poetry may open a door; concrete detail must let the reader walk through it. Avoid equations on introductory pages.

Approved names: The Light Company; Prism Robotics; Prism IRL. “Git for your physical space” is a product-direction metaphor. Do not present it as a shipped Git integration or exact physical rollback.

## Boundaries
No verified model-ranking data was supplied. The Arena-style workspace stays illustrative and noindex. Do not promote its numbers as results. Scene branching, shared review and automated regression scheduling are proposed direction. No verified open requisitions, standard prices, delivery guarantees or security certifications were supplied.

The legal pages cover the website. They do not establish an unknown legal entity, jurisdiction, enterprise data agreement or product warranty. Those details need owner/legal review before a commercial contract relies on them.

No paid advertising, outreach, social posting, Search Console submission or external analytics enrollment was performed. Distribution copy is prepared, not sent. The site's technical SEO is implemented; organic performance remains unmeasured.
