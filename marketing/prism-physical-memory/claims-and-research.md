## Research consulted

- Bifrost Manifold: https://www.bifrost.ai/ — inspected November 1, 2026. Inspiration: make an evaluation workflow concrete through candidate comparison, failure inspection and a clear next action. Distinction: their site emphasizes simulated policy evaluation; Prism's new explanation centers on physical scene preparation. No partnership or copied branding is implied.
- Google SEO Starter Guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide — clear structure, descriptive titles, useful original material and crawlable links. Applied to the hub, guides, page metadata and navigation.
- Google AI features guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features — ordinary search foundations remain relevant; a special AI file is not a ranking guarantee. llms.txt is a reading aid, not a claimed Google requirement.
- Robot Learning as an Empirical Science: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09491 — richer evaluation reporting, including conditions and success criteria. Used as primary research context, without importing unsupported performance statistics.
- Colosseum: https://robot-colosseum.github.io/ — systematic environmental perturbations in simulation; used to explain the value of named axes, not physical Prism results.
- SIMPLER: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.05941 — simulation-based evaluation against real robot setups; linked as independent reading.
- FTC business data guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business — data minimization, inventory and clear handling informed the practical privacy copy.
- FTC advertising guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/advertising-faqs-guide-small-business — factual claims should have a supporting basis. Concept and illustrative labels are retained in visible copy and metadata.
- W3C WCAG overview: https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/ — reference for accessibility improvements; no blanket conformance claim.

## Information gain over generic evaluation articles
Search results emphasize benchmark design and aggregate performance. These guides add a concrete physical-workcell thread: intended versus observed versus accepted scenes; projection visibility during recording; a downloadable planning sheet; and explicit limits of replay. This is an editorial assessment, not a claim that no other author has discussed these topics.
