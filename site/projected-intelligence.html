What it is

Projected intelligence turns the workspace into the interface.

Traditional software puts instructions on a screen. Wearable augmented reality puts them in a headset. Projected intelligence puts them on the physical work itself.

The system combines cameras, spatial understanding, task logic, and projection. It can point to a fastener, outline a placement, show the next assembly step, or stage a repeatable robotics test condition where the operator needs it.

How it works

From live scene to visible action.

  1. 01

    Observe the workspace.

    Vision identifies the person, parts, tools, surfaces, and current physical state.

  2. 02

    Understand the task.

    The system connects the live scene to the procedure, policy, or goal.

  3. 03

    Project the next move.

    Guidance appears on the exact object or area where the action happens.

  4. 04

    Verify and continue.

    The system checks the result, updates its state, and points to what comes next.
