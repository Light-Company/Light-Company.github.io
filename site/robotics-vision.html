How it works

One closed loop,
from setup to answer.

Every trial runs the same way, every time. Light shows the setup, vision checks it, a model scores it, and the console keeps it.

Stage
01 · Stage

Light shows the setup.

Every object's exact pose is projected onto the table. Operators set a scene in seconds, with no rulers and no tape.

Verify
02 · Verify

Vision checks the scene.

Identity, pose, occlusion and tolerance are confirmed before the robot moves. Bad setups never become bad data.

Run
03 · Run

Your robot, your policy.

Prism tracks the scene live while the policy runs, so the record of every trial matches what really happened.

Score
04 · Score

Every trial, scored with a reason.

Our vision-language model judges each trial against your rubric and explains why. Consistent at 3 a.m. and on run 3,000.

Compare
05 · Compare

Answers your team can ship on.

Results land in the console. Compare policies head to head, catch regressions, and see exactly where a robot fails.

Does Prism work with our robot?

Yes. Prism controls and checks the physical scene around the robot, so it works with any arm, gripper, policy or training framework. Results come into the console from the station, or from your own harness through the API.

How is a trial scored?

Our vision-language model watches each trial, judges it against your rubric and writes down why. Your team can review and override any call, and every decision is stored with its evidence.

What does it cost?

Every engagement starts with a 30-day pilot on your own robot. After that, stations are priced per workcell with the console, scoring and support included. Book a call and we will size it to your lab.

How fast can we start?

Most teams go from unboxing to scored trials in a day. We help set up your first test suite and stay on call while your team gets going.
