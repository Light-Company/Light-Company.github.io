Capability maps

Map where the robot
works and fails.

Prism changes one test condition at a time: position, orientation, clutter, objects, or light. The result shows where each policy works and fails.

Evaluation method

Compare policies under
the same physical
conditions.

Prism uses projection to stage the scene and vision to verify it. Compare Policy A with Policy B under the same physical conditions, so each result shows a policy change instead of room noise.
