How we approach the website

The company pages use semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, readable contrast and responsive layouts. Interactive controls have text labels. Motion respects the browser's reduced-motion preference where supported by the page.

The scene-history walkthrough includes a text description of each change alongside its diagram. The ASCII footer is decorative and is hidden from assistive technology.

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines as a reference for improvement. This statement is not a claim of complete conformance or an independent accessibility audit.

Known limitations

Some recorded demonstrations communicate information visually, and not every video has a complete caption or audio-description track. Some older pages and third-party players may have different accessibility behavior from the new company pages.

If a film, document or interactive view prevents you from getting the information you need, tell us which content matters. We can discuss a text explanation or another way to share it.

Report an accessibility issue

Email hi@lightcompany.ai with the page URL, what you were trying to do and what happened. If useful, include your browser or assistive technology, but do not send sensitive personal information.

We welcome specific reports about keyboard access, contrast, text size, motion, media alternatives or confusing controls. A useful website should remain useful when someone's way of using it differs from ours.
