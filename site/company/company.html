The company behind Prism.

We build evaluation tools for physical AI. Prism combines projection, perception and software so robotics teams can run repeatable tests and understand their results.

Make robot evaluations more useful.

Robotics teams need to know whether a new policy is better, where it fails and whether a result can be reproduced. That requires control over the physical setup and a clear record of what happened.

We build the station and software that connect those parts of the workflow. Operators can prepare a defined scene, researchers can compare matched conditions, and the team can review the evidence behind a result.

Our products

Prism Robotics

The evaluation platform for physical AI. Stage and verify scenes, run test suites, score rollouts, compare policies and retain the conditions behind each result.

Explore Prism Robotics.

Prism IRL

Projected interfaces for everyday surfaces. Prism combines vision and light to put information and guidance where people are working.

Explore Prism IRL.

Built across hardware, software and robotics.

Our team brings experience from Apple engineering and Carnegie Mellon research. We work across station hardware, perception, evaluation software and the operator experience.

The product is developed around real workcells and the people running them. A useful evaluation tool needs to fit the reset process, preserve the right evidence and make the next decision easier.
