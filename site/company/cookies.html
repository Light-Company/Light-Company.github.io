What the browser remembers.

A small amount of browser state supports the website. Embedded services may have their own behavior.

Last updated November 1, 2026. This page covers the public website. Product deployments may have separate written terms.

On this page

Storage used by the websiteThird-party contentYour choices

Storage used by the website

The benchmark introduction stores a dismissal flag in session storage under prism-benchmark-intro-v1. It helps avoid repeatedly showing the same introduction during a browsing session. It does not contain your name or email.

The scene-history concept has no saved account or server-side scene history. Its selected revision is held in the page while it is open. Reloading returns the example to its starting state.

Hosting and security services may use cookies or similar mechanisms for delivery, abuse prevention or access controls. Their behavior can differ from the page's own scripts.

Third-party content

The gallery uses YouTube's player technology. Loading that page or interacting with its player can contact YouTube and involve information such as your IP address and browser details. Third-party cookies and other processing are governed by the provider's settings and policies.

Some older pages may request remotely hosted fonts. New company pages use locally hosted fonts. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can remove cookies and site storage through your browser settings, block third-party cookies and choose not to load pages containing external media. Clearing storage may make an introduction appear again.

The current website code does not include advertising pixels or a marketing analytics tracker. If additional non-essential tracking is introduced, its purpose and any required choices should be provided before it is used.

For questions about personal information, see the privacy notice or contact hi@lightcompany.ai.
