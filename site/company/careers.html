The problems are wonderfully physical.

We care about the distance between a compelling demo and a tool someone uses again tomorrow. A calibration that survives the day. A record someone can understand six months later. An interface that remains clear when the room is noisy and the operator is tired.

Those problems do not belong to one discipline. The best answer may involve a mount, a camera setting, a database record, a sentence or a better question.

Where your curiosity might fit.

Perception & robotics
Registration, scene understanding, evaluation methods and the boundaries of what a camera can verify.
Systems & software
Reliable station software, understandable records, integrations and tools that help a lab recover from mistakes.
Optics & hardware
Light, cameras, mounting, thermal behavior and the practical details that turn a workbench into a dependable instrument.
Design & storytelling
Interfaces that explain their own state; films and writing that make physical intelligence tangible without overstating it.
Lab operations
Repeatable processes, clear documentation and patient observation of the difference between intended and actual work.

Bring a point of view and something to show.

A project, a repository, a paper, a physical build, a short film or a careful write-up can tell us how you think. Explain what you tried, what surprised you and what you would change.

We welcome different educational and professional paths. If a conventional résumé leaves out the most interesting part of your work, tell us that part.

Start with the work.

Email hi@lightcompany.ai with a short introduction, relevant links and the kind of contribution you want to make. Tell us your location and any practical constraints you want us to consider.

Please do not send identity documents, financial information or sensitive information about other people. If a formal opportunity opens, its responsibilities, location and terms will be discussed explicitly. We cannot promise a response or a role from an expression of interest.

Need an accessible way to share your work? Say what would help. Read our privacy notice for how contact information is handled.
