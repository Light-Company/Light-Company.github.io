The public website

The website explains our work, hosts films and educational content, and collects station-interest submissions. It is separate from a customer's operational workcell. The public scene-history concept does not connect to cameras or robot controllers.

The station-interest endpoint validates submitted fields, limits request size, checks the request origin and stores accepted submissions in a database. Those are specific implementation measures, not a claim of comprehensive security certification.

Read the privacy notice and browser-storage information for the public site's data practices.

Before a station enters your lab

A deployment discussion should establish the data and operating boundaries before access is provided. These are questions to settle together, not a list of services this website promises:

  • Which camera images, scene records and trial recordings are collected?
  • What stays on the station, what is transmitted and where is it stored?
  • Who can access the records, and how is access removed?
  • How are updates, credentials, backups and incident contacts handled?
  • What retention, export and deletion arrangements apply?
  • Which actions require operator review, and which system controls the robot?

Any customer-specific security commitments, data-processing terms, service levels or network requirements belong in the applicable written agreement.

Claims you can inspect

We distinguish recorded demonstrations, workflow descriptions, operational estimates and proposed product concepts. None should silently stand in for another.

The public benchmark scores are illustrative. Scene-history branching and collaborative workflows shown here are concepts. This site does not assert SOC 2, ISO 27001 or another certification, nor does it promise universal compatibility or a guaranteed accuracy level.

For the physical method and known constraints, read the technical brief. For a proposed deployment, ask us for evidence relevant to your actual task.

Report a security concern

Email hi@lightcompany.ai with “Security report” in the subject. Include the affected URL or component, a clear description and non-sensitive reproduction steps.

Do not include credentials, private customer data or harmful payloads in the initial message. Avoid accessing data that is not yours or disrupting a live service. This page is a reporting contact, not authorization to test private systems or a promise of a bounty.
