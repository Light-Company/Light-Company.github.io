See the physical work.

Writing can explain a method. Footage shows what a person actually does with it. Explore the lab gallery, watch the evaluation film or follow the Prism workflow from staging to review.

Our technical brief on initial-condition control describes the mechanism and its limitations. Operational figures in that brief reflect a limited partner context, not an independently validated market-wide benchmark.

Reading that shaped the questions.

Robot Learning as an Empirical Science: Best Practices for Policy Evaluation argues for richer reporting of initial conditions, success criteria and observed behavior.

Colosseum explores robustness across environmental changes in simulation. SIMPLER studies simulated evaluation against real robot setups. These are independent research projects, not Light Company results or endorsements.

Our guides connect those questions to the practical work of preparing and documenting a physical scene. They do not turn a simulated result into a claim about a physical deployment.
