How it works

Your test case.
Right on the table.

Prism connects the case in your code to the scene in front of your robot, then keeps the evidence of every run.

  1. 01

    Define and stage.

    Specify a case in code. Placements, outlines and task cues are projected so anyone can reset the scene in seconds.

  2. 02

    Verify.

    Object identity, pose and tolerance are checked before the policy runs. Bad setups never become bad data.

  3. 03

    Evaluate.

    Every trial is scored against your rubric and tied to the exact conditions that produced it.

  4. 04

    Map and retest.

    Compare models across starting conditions. Revisit failures, catch regressions, close coverage gaps.
