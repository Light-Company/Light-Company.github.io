    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    What does Prism do?+

    Prism projects the required test setup into your workcell and uses vision to check the physical scene. Your team can compare robot policies under the same starting conditions and change conditions deliberately to map performance.

    Will it work with our robot?+

    Share your robot, workcell, task, and evaluation workflow with the team. We’ll review the station layout and integration with you before the order is finalized.

    What comes with my order?+

    Your order specifies the Prism hardware, software access, setup requirements, and any deployment support. Contact the team to confirm the configuration and included services for your lab.

    When will it ship?+

    Availability, delivery timing, shipping cost, and supported destinations are confirmed for your order. For a conference, research deadline, or multi-station rollout, share your target date with the team.

    Are these photographs of the product?+

    The studio and close-up images visualize the proposed industrial design. The lab and working-prototype images show real hardware. Confirm the supplied hardware configuration when ordering.

    How do purchasing and support work?+

    Our team confirms your configuration and purchasing terms, including support, cancellation, and returns. When online checkout is available for your offer, payment is handled on Stripe’s secure checkout page.
