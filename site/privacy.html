Privacy, in plain language.

This policy explains what information we collect through lightcompany.ai, why we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective August 18, 2026

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, company, project details, or other information included in a demo, investor, support, or pre-order inquiry.

Our hosting and security providers may also process basic technical information, including IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and request logs. This helps us operate, protect, and understand the reliability of the site.

How we use information

We use information to answer inquiries, arrange demonstrations, evaluate pre-orders and partnerships, provide support, improve the site and our products, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share only what is necessary with service providers that help us host, secure, and operate the site; with professional advisers; or when required by law, safety, or a business transaction.

Cookies and site data

The marketing site does not intentionally use advertising cookies. Essential hosting, security, and media-delivery systems may use limited technical data needed to serve and protect the site.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including legal, security, and recordkeeping needs. We use reasonable safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you have provided to us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.
