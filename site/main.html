Why this matters

Stop measuring the setup. Start measuring the policy.

A few centimeters of object movement can shift measured success from roughly 95% to 50–75%. Without control and logging, the score mixes model quality with an undocumented placement distribution.

Built for credible measurement

What Prism controls—and what it does not.

Physical AI infrastructure should make its boundaries visible.

Controls

Planar placement of rigid objects, scene identity, position, condition history, and replay.

Measures next

Human placement tolerance per task and the residual variance that survives staging.

Does not solve alone

Scoring-rubric ambiguity, deformable-object state, lighting, or robot-side state.

Gated during capture

Visible projection is removed while recording so guidance does not become a visual confound.
