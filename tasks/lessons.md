# Project lessons

- Treat requests to restyle the Robotics landing as visual-only unless the user explicitly asks for copy changes. Preserve the approved headline, body copy, and CTA labels while changing composition, media treatment, or interface chrome.
- In Robotics proof sections, lead with the customer outcome and the strongest derived comparison. Keep pilot figures visibly labeled as estimates, and do not use station count as the headline proof when faster evaluations and saved engineering time are available.
- Preserve the main hero copy exactly when reshaping the home page: "The Light Company" and "AI that points to the work. No Glasses No Headset."
- Reverse only a duplicate text layer inside the moving spotlight; never invert the photograph or replace its soft feathered mask with a hard circle.
- Use the full brand name "The Light Company" in every visible Robotics-page reference and accessibility label; reserve lowercase lightcompany only for URLs and asset paths.
- For the launch selector, use one continuous 8px solid white center seam. Do not use SVG dash styling or angled geometry that stretches into fragments.
- Do not autofocus either selector card on launch. Both cards must begin in the same idle state and show the same active frame only on hover or keyboard focus.
- When asked to crop portrait videos into their display frames, preserve full playback duration and choose a focal point from the footage. Do not substitute blurred sidebars for the requested crop.
- Resolve ambiguous video swaps from the marked sections. Swap the source, poster, and video description together without changing other copies of the same media.
- Corner peels must be triangular and flush with the actual top-right page corner, not floating rectangles below navigation. Keep the crease attached and move the assembly together; reserve space for navigation and playback controls on both pages.
- Keep hosted video files below the 25 MiB static-asset limit and verify full duration after compression. Large source-history uploads can fail even when the current output is valid; use an exact-tree release snapshot based on the existing Sites head without including superseded oversized video blobs.
